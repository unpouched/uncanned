# Uncanned Support

For questions, technical issues, feedback, or privacy requests, email [unpouchedapp@gmail.com](mailto:unpouchedapp@gmail.com). This is the current support address for Uncanned.

Uncanned is provided by Sander Pärn.

## Get help with the app

You can also open **Settings → Contact Support** inside the app. If you report a technical issue, include your app version, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. The app version is shown in Settings.

## Subscriptions and purchases

Uncanned requires an active Pro subscription or trial to access the main app. An account is optional; you can subscribe without creating one.

**Restore a purchase** using **Restore Purchases** on the subscription screen. Make sure your iPhone is signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

**Cancel a subscription** on your iPhone in **Settings → your name → Subscriptions**. Select the app's subscription and choose **Cancel Subscription** or **Cancel Free Trial**. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app or your app account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

**Request a refund** through [Apple's Report a Problem website](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/). Apple handles payments, subscription cancellation, and refund decisions.

## Your data and account

**Delete your plan and logs** in the app under **Settings → Delete all my data**. This removes the app's plan and usage records from your phone and deletes the signed-in user's backup.

**Delete your account** under **Settings → Account → Delete my account**. This removes the account and its backup. Apple users may be asked to re-authorize.

If your subscription has ended, the subscription screen still offers **Delete my data** and **Sign in**. You can sign in to access account deletion.

Support correspondence is stored separately from your plan and backup. To request deletion of support messages, email the support address above.
