# Uncanned Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Uncanned helps adults cut down and quit nicotine pouches and snus. Your plan and logs are stored on your phone. Optional account backup, support requests, and usage statistics involve additional processing described below. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps for advertising.

## Who we are

Uncanned is provided by Sander Pärn ("we", "us"), the controller responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Contact: [hello@uncanned.app](mailto:hello@uncanned.app).

## Data stored on your phone

When you use Uncanned, it stores on your device:

- Your answers from setup: product, strength, pouches per day, can price, wake and sleep times, habits, triggers, reasons and an optional first name.
- Your plan, pouch and slip logs, craving sessions and ratings, daily check-ins (mood and craving), if-then plans and in-app progress.

Some of these records are health-related data. They are not automatically uploaded while you are signed out. Optional backup is described below. Any information you choose to send in a support message is processed separately. Use Settings → Delete all my data to delete the local plan and usage records; deleting the app alone does not remove server-side backups or support correspondence.

## Optional account and backup

An account is optional. You can subscribe and use the app without one. If you create an account, we process:

- Your email address, or a sign-in identifier from Apple or Google (Sign in with Apple lets you hide your email).
- An account ID.
- If backup is on, a copy of the data listed above. Backup is on by default after sign-in and can be turned off in Account.

We use this to sign you in, restore your plan on a new phone and keep your subscription with you. It is stored with our backend provider on servers in the EU, encrypted at rest. Your backup can only be accessed through your account. We do not look at it unless needed to keep the service running or required by law. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and stored in hashed form; we never see them.

Turning backup off stops new uploads. To remove the saved copy, use Delete all my data or Delete my account.

## Support messages

You can contact us by email or through **Contact Support** inside the app.

- Email support processes your email address, message, and any information you choose to include so we can respond.
- Opening in-app support starts Keepwell in support-only mode. It creates or resumes a support session using a random installation identifier and basic technical information, including device model, operating-system version, app and SDK version, language, and time zone.
- If you send a chat message, its text, conversation history, and related timestamps are stored to deliver the conversation and let us reply.
- The app does not automatically attach your pouch logs, craving ratings, health answers, or backup to support messages. The current support-only integration does not send Apple purchase history to Keepwell or enable photo attachments, promotional offers, or retention prompts.

Support correspondence is separate from your app account and backup. **Delete all my data** and **Delete my account** do not automatically delete support conversations. To request access to or deletion of support correspondence, email [hello@uncanned.app](mailto:hello@uncanned.app).

## Usage statistics

If "Share usage stats" is on (default on, you can turn it off in Settings), the app sends anonymous events: which setup screens are reached, whether the paywall was shown, which plan was selected, whether a purchase was started, cancelled, failed, completed or restored, whether the App Store could not be reached, whether the screens shown after a trial ends, after a purchase, or when you return without subscribing appeared, whether you allowed or declined notifications during setup, which of four fixed answers you picked if the app asked why you switched off renewal (or that you skipped it), and where you heard about the app. Events carry no values: no prices, no error text, no receipts and no identifiers we choose.

These events do not include pouch counts, nicotine amounts, triggers, reasons, health answers, your name, or your email. TelemetryDeck processes technical app/device information and pseudonymous identifiers to produce usage statistics. The analytics setting controls those statistics; it does not disable account, purchase, or support processing needed for the features you use.

## Optional PostHog analytics

PostHog Cloud EU receives no events until you enable the relevant purpose in **Privacy & analytics**, available before subscribing and in Settings. Both purposes are off by default. Your existing master analytics opt-out remains effective.

- **Onboarding and checkout diagnostics**: setup screen visits and active viewing time, plan-save results, store offer loading and technical timings, purchase and restore attempts and their bounded outcomes, observed access changes, authentication session-restore completion and backup-operation outcomes. Purchase outcomes distinguish cancellation, pending approval, errors and access being granted; they are not a record of money received.
- **Optional app activity**: first successfully saved useful action, one useful day per wake-to-wake plan day, and visits to Today, Progress, Plan, Your journey, Ride It Out and the widget guide. Supported actions are a saved log or eligible wait, a full three-minute Ride It Out, or a saved check-in. We do not send the action type, counts, ratings or plan-day key. Widget and shortcut actions performed outside the app are not measured.

These interactions can reveal that you use a nicotine quitting app. Each event uses a random analytics reference, occurrence time, app version/build, distribution and whether consent began before or after a local plan existed. Diagnostic properties are restricted to stable screen numbers, duration, fixed outcome/provider/context codes, public product IDs and random attempt/presentation IDs. We do not send your name, email, Supabase or RevenueCat identifier, Apple transaction or receipt, pouch counts, nicotine amounts, triggers, health answers, craving ratings, exact plan dates or detailed progress. No screen recordings, automatic interaction capture, advertising identifier or cross-company tracking. PostHog is not linked to your account, purchase identity or TelemetryDeck identifier.

Requests reach PostHog's EU ingestion service. Internet transport exposes your IP to network infrastructure; the integration disables event geolocation enrichment and the project discards client IP data. This does not mean network infrastructure never sees an IP address.

Turning a purpose off stops its new events and removes its unsent queued events. The master switch stops all new analytics. Events already transmitted cannot be recalled by cancelling a request. Consent choices and the analytics reference stay on your phone through **Delete all my data** so your choice and erasure lookup are retained. No earlier actions are backfilled when you opt in.

To request deletion of previously uploaded PostHog events, open **Privacy & analytics → Prepare analytics deletion request**, then send the prepared email to support. Preparing it pauses PostHog and clears pending events; the email still needs to be sent and support must complete deletion. Keep the displayed reference until fulfillment. Local data deletion, account deletion and consent withdrawal do not automatically erase uploaded analytics. If you remove the app, keep your reference first: we cannot look up this separate analytics history using your account email.

## Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details. To check whether you have Pro, our subscription provider (RevenueCat) receives an anonymous app ID (or your account ID if you are signed in) and your purchase history from Apple.

If "Share usage stats" is on, your answer to "Where did you find us?" during setup (for example "a friend" or the name of a platform) is also stored with the subscription provider as one attribute called `source`, next to that anonymous app ID. It tells us which channels lead to subscriptions. It contains no pouch, health, or plan data. Changing your answer replaces it; with the setting off it is not sent.

## Notifications

Plan reminders are scheduled locally on your phone and respect your notification settings and sleep schedule. If you start a free trial and notifications are allowed, the app also schedules one local reminder two days before the trial ends. It states the first charge and its date as reported by the App Store. You can turn it off in Settings.

If you allowed notifications during setup and saved a plan but have not subscribed, the app schedules at most two local reminders that your plan is saved: one the next morning at your wake time and one three days later. They stop when you subscribe and are never repeated. On the first morning of a running plan the app sends one local notification that the day has started. When a subscription or trial is not set to renew, the app schedules one local notification for the time access ends, saying your plan is paused and saved. All of these are created on your phone; none is sent from a server.

## Why we may use your data (legal basis)

- Health-related data: your explicit consent, given when you hold to commit during setup and when you keep backup on. You can withdraw it by turning backup off and deleting your data or account.
- Account, backup and purchases: to provide the service you asked for.
- TelemetryDeck funnel statistics: our legitimate interest in improving the app. You can turn them off.
- Optional PostHog diagnostics and app activity: your separate consent for each purpose. Activity can reveal use of a nicotine quitting app; the separate activity toggle requests explicit consent for this health-related inference. You can withdraw either choice in Privacy & analytics without affecting access.
- Support correspondence and technical support context: to respond to requests you make and our legitimate interest in resolving problems and providing customer support.
- Where the law requires us to keep or share data.

## Who we share data with

Only service providers that run parts of the app for us:

- Supabase: accounts and backup.
- TelemetryDeck: funnel statistics.
- PostHog Cloud EU: separately consented diagnostics and app activity.
- RevenueCat: subscription status.
- Keepwell: in-app support sessions and messages.
- Our email service: messages you send to the support address.
- GitHub Pages: hosting this public support and policy website.
- Apple and Google: sign-in and payments, only if you use them.

They may use the data only to provide their service to us. If a provider processes data outside the EU/EEA, it is protected by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We never sell or rent your data.

## How long we keep it

- On your phone: until you delete it or the app.
- Account and backup: until you delete your data or your account in the app. Copies in our providers' backups are removed within their normal cycle.
- TelemetryDeck statistics: processed using pseudonymous identifiers and aggregated reports.
- PostHog events: project retention is set to three months. Cleanup runs monthly and deletes whole months, so some events can remain up to about one additional month. Unsent events on your phone expire after seven days; the queue holds at most 200 events and is excluded from device backup. Erasure requests are handled separately from retention.
- Support correspondence: retained while needed to handle your request, follow up, and meet applicable record-keeping requirements. You can request deletion by contacting us. Some records may need to be retained where the law requires it.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time. Most of this you can do in the app. For anything else, email us at hello@uncanned.app and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country. In Estonia this is the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, www.aki.ee).

## Age

Uncanned is for adults aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 has used the app, contact us and we will delete their data.

## Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your data, such as encryption and access controls. No system is completely secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
